# Current experimental NVIDIA provider: evidence and open issues

This addendum describes CAD source candidate `3bd196f3102db48ddeca5db458f6fb7969706c4c`. It selects the guarded experimental direct-NVIDIA SMLib provider containing kernel change `6c99bfcfe6c7b9735a8c617b4207921c64b0555e`. It does **not** establish deployment, broad native correctness or production readiness. The application default remains OCC. Direct-app and signed final-candidate tests are separate work; this note does not claim their result.

## What changed and what remains guarded

The earlier third-party wrapper treated a valid NVIDIA edge-contact union as a generic engine failure when the whole result was nonmanifold. The raw-provider path instead keeps the whole native BRep and reports `empty`, `single-material` or `multi-material`, with native validity and material/void census kept separate. It preserves a single-material cavity as its whole BRep and does not invent a manifold body or manufacture grouping. Nonmanifold native volume is unavailable (`null`): NVIDIA documents that volume as undefined. Display meshes remain display-only. This removes the earlier wrapper's false assumption for supported outputs; it does not prove all multi-body or nonmanifold operations correct.

The provider still explicitly rejects a result with **more than one material region and at least one void** as `MULTI_MATERIAL_VOID_UNSUPPORTED`, closing the disposable candidate Worker. It retains native level-0 and level-2 validity checks and fails closed on rejected operations. A rejected candidate does not imply that an independently accepted mount is invalid. No general nested-region normalization, operand reversal or mesh-derived native volume is applied.

## Corrected exact fills; partial fill still fails native validity

With A=`[0,0,0]+[10,10,10]`, I=`[2,2,2]+[6,6,6]`, H=A−I, the older direct native kernel returned an order-dependent, incorrectly classified result for H∪I: zero material, one void, native volume zero, despite the expected filled box. The experimental `6c99` change corrects the tested exact fill to one material, zero voids and native volume 1000. It also corrects tested enclosing and disconnected-composite fills in both relevant orders without a new regression in the fixed corpus. The targeted actual provider test checks H∪I and verifies A, I and H exposed evidence and fixed-tolerance mesh hashes remain unchanged; a separate accepted mount survives rejected partial-fill work. See [provider test and receipt](../../test/raw-smlib-6c/README.md).

The independently computed [48-expression integer-box cell oracle](../../test/native-box-oracle/README.md) compares the old native build against `6c99` without relying on tessellation: **6 corrected, 34 passing on both, 2 unchanged failures, 6 boundary-only not-qualified cases and 0 new regressions**. Thus the candidate has 40 passes and 2 failures among 42 scored volumetric cases; it is not an all-pass result. The unchanged failures are forward H∪P and its translation, with P=`[2,2,2]+[3,6,6]`: oracle and native volume agree at 892 with one material and one void, but the oracle's actual query path reports native level-0 and level-2 validity false. Reversed order and the recorded cyclic coordinate permutation pass. An earlier direct diagnostic observed a different level-0 value under a different query path; that discrepancy does not establish a cause. No validity failure is replaced by a mesh-derived acceptance.

## Separate material-side tessellation issue

For H plus a 2³ island at `[3,3,3]` inside its void, the cell oracle independently expects and observes native volume 792, two material interiors and one void. The returned raw triangle order, however, has three closed components with signed integrals approximately +1000, −216 and **−8**, totaling 776. In a separate direct NVIDIA baseline diagnostic, analytically known box points and native region classifications identify the island as material. Orienting the 12 island triangles toward their sampled void side produces +1000, −216 and +8, totaling 792, for both operand orders. This is a bounded demonstration of the orientation problem, not a safe general tessellation algorithm or a reason to trust a mesh integral as native volume.

The same diagnostic found two reversed partial-fill triangles; fixing their orientation in the diagnostic produced a closed paired-edge mesh with sum 892, but does not cure the independent forward native validity failure. Returned PolyBrep back-pointer validity was false. Source-face values were compared only as labels against a still-live input face list; simple upward/downward region flags did not consistently determine raw triangle direction. No stale pointer was dereferenced and no fixed-offset sampling was adopted in the product. The direct source, before/after receipts, point/face-use and edge-incidence analysis are in NVIDIA fork branch `research/native-tess-orientation-20261105`, commit `1c2659a49efafdff0f2f7ed31ef03b27322164e8`, `research/wasm-solid-contact-20261105/TESS-ORIENTATION.md`.
