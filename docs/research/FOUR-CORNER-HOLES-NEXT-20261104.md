# Four corner mounting holes: bounded next step

This is a proposed design and test queue, not an implemented feature or a replacement for CAD Gate 1. The target is the existing worker-authored XY rectangle prism **with its separate centered through-hole still present**, followed by one additional authored feature expressing four corner mounting holes. A resize of the original rectangle must recompute all four positions; saved native history carries intent, not resolved coordinates, native handles or response-local topology indices. The existing OCC worker is the initial engine. Native Mesh and cross-engine operands must refuse until independently designed and qualified.

## Reusable evidence and actual boundaries

- The current centered-hole path supplies a bounded solved rectangle profile, candidate owner, same-mount OCC cut, analytic circles/cylinder and oriented coedges, operation-local Boolean ancestry, model projection, publication guard and history replay. Its planner accepts only principal-XY, positive-depth, bounded rectangles and one center-derived hole; its strict topology reader and drilled projection/invariant explicitly expect one hole (7 faces, 15 edge groups, 10 vertices and two rims). Extend via a distinct versioned contract rather than relaxing those checks globally.
- `candidatePrismLedger.mjs` associates only the original projected prism with its private mount/handle/source; `executeCenteredHole` keeps the resulting cut handle private in its publication closure. `worker-controller.mjs` stores the cut result in `holeRecords`, while `centeredThroughHole` requires a prism `portableRecords` parent. Another current centered-hole call cannot correctly operate on the already-drilled result, and the command inspector advertises only an undrilled `worker-owned-occ-prism`. A checked dependent-result association and new worker operation are required; feeding the original prism to a second cut would erase the first hole.
- The headless sketch solver has a separate research example with four circles constrained by eight `DistancePL` relationships. The actual 100×50→150×30 solve moves centers to `(10,10)`, `(140,10)`, `(140,20)`, `(10,20)`. Its point-to-line equations describe **infinite supporting lines**, not finite-edge containment or solid clearance, and its legacy sketch-on-face evidence does not authenticate the new worker-owned drilled solid. Reuse residual/admission and stale-boundary controls, not legacy model handles.
- `research/feature-pattern-cut-20261104` (`4fd20b69`) is a separate legacy OCC research operation: a one-axis signed-spacing pattern of the immediately preceding supported seed, count 2–16. It captures a replay-local pre-Boolean tool and uses legacy model/command names; it neither expresses symmetric offsets from all four corners nor establishes worker-owned topology ancestry. Its no-output/sentinel, collision, invalid-pattern and failure tests are useful controls. Its documented native name-allocation leak and unqualified handle lifetime remain limitations; do not transplant it as a worker pattern.
- `research/plate-parametric-20261104` and `research/plate-fillet-pattern-20261104` contain earlier five-hole, parent-resize, rays, volume and save/reopen scenarios using a different legacy path. The former documents an original partial-result failure and a later research fix; current atomic craft replay must be tested directly with the new feature. Fillet and general pattern acceptance remain separate.

## First authored operation

Use one explicitly versioned **four-corner through-hole set** feature, after the centered hole, rather than silently creating four unrelated fixed-coordinate cuts. Candidate intent includes a new feature ID; the exact current body and semantic LID, originating rectangle/prism feature and canonical authored region; the immediately preceding centered-hole feature/result as supported parent; positive `offsetX`, `offsetY` and common `diameter` in millimeters; and explicit `four-corners`/`through-all` relations. Canonical corner roles are `MIN_X_MIN_Y`, `MAX_X_MIN_Y`, `MAX_X_MAX_Y`, `MIN_X_MAX_Y` in the qualified world-XY frame. This first operation is not a general pattern, an editable circle sketch, arbitrary datum direction or variable count. A later UI and typed command should submit the same validated feature with revision/lifetime guards and discovered IDs; no generic `craft.modify`, worker names or handles.

Derive each center from the **current re-solved parent rectangle**, by offsetting the two identified supporting sides inward and intersecting them. Verify unique corner/side association, orientation and finite-edge/face containment against the actual unsnapped rectangle. Classify sides/corners only within the existing principal-XY contract; refuse changed type, correspondence, direction or branch. Do not select a replacement edge by nearest position or persist a previous center. Validate finite positive diameter and offsets, precision/domain bounds, strict clearance of each entire circle from every finite side, and strict pairwise separation from all four new circles **and the existing center hole**. Preserve parent depth/normal and use actual analytic distances, with numerical clearance tolerance stated separately from manufacturing clearance.

A useful non-touching acceptance fixture is parent 100×50×20, center diameter 10, four diameter-4 corner holes at offsets 10×10; resize parent to 150×30×35. Expected new centers are `(10,10)`, `(140,10)`, `(140,20)`, `(10,20)` and center remains `(75,15)`. On a 30 mm high rectangle, four **diameter-10** holes at those offsets touch their neighbors: center distance is 10 mm and the radius sum is 10 mm. That input must refuse under strict-separation semantics; a test must not hide this with a loose tolerance. Diameter 4 is a test choice, not a manufacturing-fit recommendation.

## Changes and dependency order

1. **Independent authored contract and planner tests.** Define canonical feature/parent references, units, domains, corner order, side provenance, exact in/out schema, tolerance and pairwise/edge checks. Add a pure data-only planner for four holes against the copied worker-owned rectangle profile. Test translated origins, both sizes/depths, dimension/diameter edits, out-of-face/touching/overlap with center and each other, zero/nonfinite, tiny/degenerate and high-coordinate inputs, ambiguous sides and changed source revision. Independently verify analytic clearance; no snapping. This can run in parallel with history schema/command planning.
2. **Worker ownership and real cut.** Extend the candidate-local ledger and mount with a bounded association for the **checked centered-hole result** and original prism profile/feature identity. Retain handles only in the same live candidate owner/mount; recheck binding, prior projection and source before/after every await and at publication. A new bounded same-mount operation consumes that exact drilled result, executes four real cylindrical cuts without stale-name reuse, and produces a fresh valid single solid or terminates the candidate. Evaluate a non-destructive multi-tool cut or another explicitly checked equivalent; no implementation is presumed. Account for temporary kernel names, existing eight-name reservations, 30-second worker bounds, output limits and whole-worker termination; do not raise limits silently or claim native memory reclamation. Preserve the previously accepted owner during evaluation.
3. **Topology, history and projection.** Add a versioned multi-hole response and strict bounded validation: analytic planes/circles/cylinders; both sets of planar inner loops; shared edges, orientations and repeated same-face seams; one-solid/BRep and independent mesh/volume/bounds checks. Identify every inherited feature face and center-hole wall/rim via same-operation history and verify each new corner wall/rim through cutter/support-face ancestry; resolve uniquely, or refuse split/merged/ambiguous/deleted references. Existing prism and centered-hole identities may survive only when proven. New keys derive from this feature and corner labels, not result order or rounded coordinates. Unqualified edges/vertices/seams stay transient. A simple result may have 11 faces, 27 edge groups and 18 vertices, but measure and justify actual OCC output; these counts are not universal. Audit display winding, circular loops, registry collisions and mutation/lifetime invariants for the enlarged bounded model.
4. **History/command/UI integration.** Independently prepare a fixed schema and discoverable typed command, with current revision, exact parent/body/LID and representation checks, feature-ID allocation, and the same guarded craft operation as its visible action. Prototype with test doubles if useful, but remain unregistered/unexposed until steps 1–3 are reviewed. Replay consumes the exact centered-hole model and atomically publishes body/history/registry; native save/reopen stores only intent. Do not infer that the current centered-hole command can operate on its own drilled output.
5. **End-to-end and recovery.** Exercise actual sketch/worker/native/model/history path, supported visible and typed-command entries, native save/reopen and separately signed My files when packaged. Resize width/height and depth, alter offsets and diameter, undo/redo, suppress/delete/repair dependencies, and verify meaningful face/hole roles. Independently check all five openings and material between/near them via ray/section tests and analytic/OCC volume, reporting triangulation error separately. Force stale revision/body, wrong engine/mount, overlapping/touching/outside circles, kernel success-without-output, malformed topology/history, registry collision, worker death, timeout, host/account closure and late cancellation. Rejected edits must preserve the actual accepted history/model/registry/sketch and owner according to the history UI's explicit rewind behavior, or label a failed authored replay stale while retaining last-complete geometry. No partial set may become current.

Planner, contract and history/command tests can run independently. Native worker, checked ancestry and projection each have separable unit work but actual adapter/publication depend on all three agreeing on the same verified response. Fillets, patterns, manufacturing export, physics, assembly and the remaining Gate 1 checks are not satisfied by this milestone.
