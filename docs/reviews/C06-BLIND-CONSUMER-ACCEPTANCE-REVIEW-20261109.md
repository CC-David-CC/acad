## Material acceptance/custody findings

### 1. Export and negative-file submissions lack durable-settlement custody

`test/centered-blind-hole-workflow/desktop-driver.mjs:53`, `exports`, submits STEP, 3MF and SVG before readback. None sets `durablePending`. If any submission rejects with unknown/possibly-committed outcome, or acknowledged bytes fail to synchronize, the top-level catch is followed by `finally` retiring the owned context because that flag remains false. Earlier successful exports in the same expression may also have committed. Recording the error does not establish settlement. `save:48` has the expected pending guard; exports do not inherit it.

The own malformed-file loop likewise calls `mirrorFileContext().files.write` without a pending guard or browser/server settlement record, and subsequently opens that path. A rejected/uncertain write can also reach unconditional retirement. These are real driver control-flow gaps; no exception or race was injected or observed.

Before execution, use the existing custody convention for **each** submitted write/export: set pending before submission, retain the actual returned/error outcome promptly, await the intended own-file settlement/readback, then clear it. Process exports sequentially so each outcome is settled before starting another. On an unknown outcome retain the owned window/context and record exact pending operation/path; do not blindly retry, force close or infer rollback. No Files/product rewrite is needed. Preserve all physical criteria.

### 2. 3MF oracle does not distinguish the required circular pocket from another equal-volume recess

`check.py:93–113`, `mesh`, checks mesh indices, oriented edge usage, global bounds and total signed volume. Its vertex-domain predicate at108 takes the minimum distance to **infinite** outer/top/floor/cylinder supports. Every vertex on either top or floor is admitted regardless of radial position or triangle interior. It does not prove the circular wall, entry opening, floor disk, complete patch coverage, mesh connectedness, or triangle-domain membership.

A static counterexample to the inference is a different rectangular blind recess of equal cross-sectional area and depth: all its recess vertices can lie exactly on the admitted top/floor planes, its closed oriented mesh can have the expected total volume and outer bounds, yet it is not the circular pocket. No such mesh was generated or submitted. This establishes a limitation in the check, not a defect in actual Native/3MF output.

Strengthen the finite artifact oracle before claiming circular blind mesh geometry: independently classify complete triangles/patches with bounded analytic domains, require actual entry rim/cylindrical wall/floor coverage and correct orientation/connectivity, and retain a declared tessellation-error budget. An independent artifact point/material check can corroborate but must not replace full patch/domain obligations. Current BRep Native point observations and exact volume do not themselves certify that the separately exported mesh contains the same geometry. Do not relax the target to volume-only success.

The same function merely requires `codi:SourceRevision` to exist; it does not compare its value or provenance revision against the actual captured export input. Bind that identity as well as existing body/producer/material/resource IDs when refreshing the finite oracle.

### 3. STEP finite reader ignores parsed LINE geometry consistency

`step_oracle.py:curve` parses LINE origin, direction and scale. Later edge/cap/side checks use its `kind` plus topological vertex coordinates, but do not establish that the actual edge endpoints lie on that LINE or that each edge belongs to its actual face support. The cylindrical seam is also checked by endpoint heights/reference reuse without validating its parsed LINE geometry. Changing a LINE direction/origin while retaining the topological endpoints is therefore not detected by the relevant code. No file mutation/execution was performed.

This reader is explicitly a finite independent analytic STEP21 subset, which is an appropriate scope; that scope still needs consistency between its supported analytic geometry and topology. Check actual supported LINE endpoint incidence/scale/nondegeneracy, face-support incidence and the supported loop/edge/bound orientation semantics. Retain actual Circle/rim/support/floor checks. Do not claim general STEP validity or substitute `MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP` naming/edge census as a material-manifold proof. The production Native validity and point results remain separate observations.
