source/history/currentness and W02 request/retirement concepts: captured accepted
source → private proof → mutable candidate → guarded publication or retirement.

second state inventory, journal, owner flag or provider.

## Human policy and closed eligibility are separate boundaries

Sharing actual native schema/math preparation does not authorize imposing a new
closed-workflow whitelist on the active human editor. Compare the OLD real human
call path before extraction: actual ground/prior-stage generated references,
fresh consecutive N-selection groups, stage0 floor, existing rejected-row
recovery and original dialog constraint/annotations may have different valid
admission policies. Preserve legitimate behavior or justify a deliberate contract
repair with source/evidence. Do not narrow it merely to make an ordinary closed
fixture easier. Fixed's initial extraction and the initial PL packet both exposed
this same review issue; their corrective source commits are retained separately.

Put ordinary nongenerated/root/inventory restrictions in CLOSED eligibility,
then share genuine actual-object/schema/constant/domain preparation. Stateful
human insertion keeps its authoritative native transaction/recovery semantics;
closed mutation keeps strict accepted-source, original-request proof and one
full-suffix publication. A prior-rejected human draft row is not permission to
adopt invalid closed source. Keep the actual edited constraint and annotations
when a dialog authors them; do not silently reconstruct a metadata-free copy.

## Capture and model ownership

Use current inspected IDs and revision-bound discovery, never parsed ID grammar,
coordinates as identity, caller flags or Native ordinals. At every public realm
boundary, capture exact bounded own-data fields into fresh local immutable data.
For an array require `Array.isArray`, exact length/key census and every dense
index's own enumerable data descriptor. Do not invoke foreign iterators/getters.
For plain records use the existing realm-root/null-prototype convention plus
exact own-data keys; local `Object.prototype` equality rejects legitimate host
records. Preserve original request order/spelling; symmetric no-ops return the
actual stored row order instead of rewriting intent.

## Pure original inspection is not mutable preparation

`web/app/sketcher/io.ts:evaluateCapturedSketch` starts a one-use private decode
transaction, invokes its synchronous visitor, then discards it WITHOUT optimizer
or `finishTransaction`. Its selector may inspect real objects/Params, construct
a detached genuine-schema proposal, resolve constants, validate equations and
collect polynomials. It must not call a mutable prepare API, `_add`, solve,
start/finish a transaction, normalize geometry or publish. No public skip flag.

`actions/pointCoincidenceAdmission.js:inspectPointCoincidence` illustrates this
seam. `preparePointCoincidence` retains the normal `pm.inTransaction` refusal;
human and mutable Worker callers own a separate genuine transaction and rollback.
Preserve actual stage/root/parameter/generator ownership and aliases. Reuse
`headless/capturedParameterOwnership.ts` for descendants/factory auxiliaries;
serialized “owned” or “solved” booleans cannot replace genuine private witnesses.

Before collecting EACH actual original or proposed row's polynomials, resolve
its constants using `c.resolveConstants(pm.constantResolver)` and check its real
registered domain/original equations (`sketchValidation.ts:validateConstraintGeometry`
and schema-specific direction checks). Decoding does not initialize
`resolvedConstants`; the later shared scope pass cannot rescue an earlier selector
read. A proposed row may have an unsatisfied original-coordinate residual;
handle that through its authenticated moving branch, without suppressing domain
failures or skipping original-row validation. Retain raw names/expressions, units
and bindings—never seed a numeric cache
or replace a formula with its present value.

## Authoritative rows, admission and proof

After genuine admission, obtain the appended row from the actual IO-serialized
`warm.stages[stage].constraints[index]`. Verify real type/stage/ordered refs and
schema fields; copy that canonical row, including annotations/admission. Do not
insert raw `constraint.write()` own-undefined membership into a JSON candidate
or invent `{}` for an optional zero-constant field. An absent field or genuine
empty data record can mean no constants; null/false/array/nonempty data cannot.

`parametric.ts:finishTransaction` calls owned `AlgNumSystem.finishTransaction`
before validation. Successful fast and normal `rebuildAdmission` paths call
`recordAdmissionHints`, deriving authored status from actual redundant/conflicting
sets; IO serializes `sc.write()`. Preserve this path, then require fresh cold and
captured-equation evidence. Never manufacture manual admission classifications.

The first Worker owns the ORIGINAL request/source, actual equation-dependent
movement, exact unrelated geometry/retained intent and original-equation/domain
proof. A second disposable Worker reconstructs that unchanged original request
and binds candidate AND actual receipt/row/no-op metadata. Candidate-only replay,
structural data substitution, self-membership or matching residual arrays is not
independent intent proof. Keep existing replay/branch/unit policies; coordinate
budgets do not relax radii/angles, minima or source identity. The shared coordinator
still guards document/source/frame/history/J/lifetime around awaits and one
full-suffix publication. Attempt every candidate retirement on failure/cancellation;
unconfirmed cleanup remains explicit and cannot be reported as clean release.

## Qualification and domain limits

Complete coherent source first, then root's frozen FULL registered public
workflow, then an independent AFTER-WHOLE source/archive/artifact oracle. No
microtests/probes, fake positive model/native replies or source seeds substitute
for that chain. Retain failed attempts; keep any approved oracle-contract
correction separate from product and geometry criteria. See
[Coincidence qualification](../../test/point-coincidence-public-workflow/QUALIFICATION.md)
and [its actual R4 artifact oracle](../../test/point-coincidence-public-workflow/evidence-r4/independent-oracle-r1.json).

Source point equations, finite local rank/nullity, numerical admission diagnostics
and Native BRep/material observations are different evidence domains. Exact
rational rank of one anchored point formulation is not global physical DOF;
numerical failure is not global inconsistency. Source coincidence is not identity
welding or Native topology equivalence. A scoped complete workflow is not a full
exercise/competency/engine signoff, and unresolved candidate work gains no promise
from these guardrails.
