## Actual transition omission

Both `proof` functions call `cold` **before** copying actual returned classifications into the candidate. `cold` delegates to `solveSketch`; `solve.ts` reconstructs and solves the original registered equations and checks exact `incomingIntent(solved) === incomingIntent(input)`. `sketchValidation.ts:incomingIntent` includes the authored admission records. Therefore a necessary redundant→accepted transition cannot be authorized by the later `classify(candidate, evidence)` call: a cold reconstruction that changes that state fails first. This is the same source ordering omitted by the former LengthFamily removal path. The strict read/replay check is intentional and must stay strict.

`AlgNumSystem.rebuildAdmission` retries retained redundant rows after accepted rows; a prior redundant hint is not a permanent exemption. `parametric.ts` / `AlgNumSystem.removeConstraint`, `startTransaction`, `finishTransaction` already provide real removal/readmission/rollback. The corrected LengthFamily removal at `lengthFamilyTransition.ts` lines40–55 obtains those actual classifications before cold proof and constructs the published source from original geometry plus exactly the requested row deletion.

This establishes a source-level missing transition, conditional on a surviving row actually needing promotion. It does not establish the exact future public error code, numerical outcome or successful classification of every proposed dependency.
