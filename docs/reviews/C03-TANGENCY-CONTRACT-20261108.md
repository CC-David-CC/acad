## Original supporting-line math and branch

Let A=(ax,ay), C=(cx,cy), θ be the actual Segment angle Param, n=(-sinθ,cosθ), s=`inverted ? -1 : +1`, radius r. The original equation is

`F = n·(C−A) − s*r = 0` mm.

Its derivatives, in actual native parameter order `[θ,ax,ay,cx,cy,r]`, are

`[−cosθ*(cx−ax)−sinθ*(cy−ay), sinθ, −cosθ, −sinθ, cosθ, −s]`.

Do not replace this by squared unsigned distance, endpoint-relative cross sign, DIRECTION_SIGN(T), absolute radius or an automatically toggled `inverted`. Initial human side is `n·C < n·A`; shared numeric creation should require an explicit inspected polar-normal side and persist the real literal. Inspect actual θ/T, A/B and n in a disposable Worker, with original row/Params/ownership and current revision. Names must say **polar-normal-positive/negative**, not endpoint-relative left/right. No second persisted frame or hidden chart flag.

At θ=0,T>0,A=(0,0),B=(10,0), C=(5,5),r5,inverted=false: F=cy−5. At the equivalent private θ=0,T<0 chart for reversed endpoints, n still points+Y, unlike the endpoint-derived normal. Cold native load initializes θ from A→B. Therefore any mutation/replay implementation must prove actual original equations and side in BOTH candidate and cold reconstruction. The existing PL source-frame lifecycle is relevant, but a successful PL workflow does not certify tangency. Actual side-locked reversal can be contradictory if the center is fixed; a free center can move to the retained chart's other physical side. Preserve the literal and report the actual result rather than choosing one arbitrary free solution as analytic truth.

## Finite contact is not endpoint coincidence

For actual solved support F, derive `Q=C−(n·(C−A))*n`. Segment finite membership uses D=B−A and λ=((Q−A)·D)/(D·D), requiring0≤λ≤1 with independently validated residual/error bounds. Report whether Q is endpoint/interior and retain both endpoints. A supporting line at y0 with finite Segment x10..20 and C=(0,5),r5 is support-tangent but NOT finite-contact. No endpoint may be moved or made coincident just to change this result.

For Arc, additionally prove Q is on its actual saved CCW a→b interval with actual ArcConsistency/radius and real branch/full-turn semantics. Required positives include contact in the interior of a nonfull quarter Arc and at its actual endpoint; required negative has the same supporting circle but excludes Q. Use original vector/incidence/interval logic and bounded numerical qualification, never picking tolerance, shortest-arc inference or sampled containment. Unknown full/near-seam/native carry encodings must be explicitly unsupported if their actual branch cannot be established. This is not evidence of invalid mathematical geometry.

The proposed finite schema adds a domain/branch obligation, not an artificial tangential location equation or a hidden solver inequality engine. If the original solver reaches only a support-tangent solution outside the finite domain, refuse that candidate truthfully; do not call the mathematical request infeasible or silently add a tangential lock. Ordinary positive finite cases and later edits staying inside must still succeed. A generally free inequality-feasibility algorithm is not implied by this first sufficient domain.

## Genuine Circle-Circle equation and domain proposal

Let d=|CB−CA|. External R=rA+rB. For either internal branch use signed R=rA−rB and a retained strict containing-radius order. Polynomial `G=(bx−ax)^2+(by−ay)^2−R^2=0` has mm² units. Derivatives are `[−2dx,−2dy,2dx,2dy,−2R,−2R]` for external; the final rB derivative is `+2R` for internal. Scope consists only of the actual two centers and radius Params.

Normalize original measured residual by `d+abs(R)` to obtain radial error mm, following the existing DistancePP style; never relabel mm² as mm. Both radii must satisfy the existing positive ordinary envelope. For internal, require the declared strict containing order and nonzero center separation; equal-radius coincident circles are not unique tangency. Near singular/too-small separation may be explicitly outside the existing supported numerical domain; do not invent a new positive dimension cap or infer impossibility. Original polynomial/radii/order/domain are validated before publication and Open, not merely a derived contact-point claim.

Independent analytic contact for external is `CA + rA*(CB−CA)/d`; for A-contains-B it is the same expression; for B-contains-A it is `CA − rA*(CB−CA)/d`. The two circular radial vectors must be opposed (external) or same-directed (internal) at the actual contact. Tangent orientation does not join endpoints or imply trimmed-Arc contact. Initial branch cases below avoid d0 and unequal-radius ambiguity; coincident equal circles explicitly refuse unique tangency. This new schema's exact name/constants/domain require root review, not an already implemented capability claim.
