one current OCC build, one authored graph and the existing shared journal.

entry**, strictly less than the current part thickness. Equality/oversize refuse;
no implicit conversion to through-all, clipping, floor snapping or changed kind.

with its retained independent source, one exact four-Segment rectangle/no source
holes, positive materialized thickness T and genuine construction provenance.

from its AABB or volume. Root may approve later target families separately. The
entry is the actual unique current `last-cap` of that selected constructor;
source/datum orientation is retained. Top means +source normal, not global +Z.
The source region, live body, producer, frame and exact build must all agree.
No count-based face choice, opaque-ID parsing, nearest point, display ray or
caller-supplied solved frame can establish the entry or floor.

The center is derived from the current four source vertices as their rectangular
midranges, with the existing exact-axis/source domain and provider clearance
policy reused. All original source curves/IDs/equations/frames remain authored;
no generated tool geometry is inserted into the sketch or history. A parent
width/height edit moves the centered hole by that same declared centering rule.
A thickness change moves the top and floor while retaining the authored depth.

Proposed exact saved feature type `WORKER_CENTERED_BLIND_HOLE_V1`, parameters:

```js
{version:1,unit:'millimeter',engine:'occ',engineRevision,
```

`diameter` and `depth` reuse current authored-length capture/materialization:
